Process

How a guide project works

01

Talk it through

We start over email or a call so I can understand the situation, audience, and what the guide needs to help people do.

02

Find the source

I figure out whether we use existing notes, videos, screenshots, old docs, research, or questions to shape the material.

03

Build the guide

I organize the explanation into clear sections, steps, visuals, and supporting details.

04

Review and finish

You review the draft, I make revisions, and the final guide is ready to send, share, scan, or hand off.
